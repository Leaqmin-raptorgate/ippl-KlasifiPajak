# Demo KlasifiPajak

Use a fresh Compose volume and the academic 2026 ruleset. From the repository root:

```sh
docker compose -p kpa down -v   # reset: discards the demo database volume
docker compose -p kpa up -d --build mysql migrate api frontend
docker compose -p kpa exec -T api python backend/scripts/seed_demo.py
```

Open `http://127.0.0.1:5173`. The accounts and passwords are synthetic. The free account is `gratis@klasifipajak.demo` / `DemoGratis10`; the paid account is `berbayar@klasifipajak.demo` / `DemoBayar10`. The seed is repeatable for a fresh database. Its paid access is for demonstration, not evidence of a payment. The dashboard figures below describe the paid account before adding or editing its transactions, with the seeded 2026 rows on or before the current Jakarta date. Restart with a fresh volume for the same figures after changing data.

1. **Onboarding and guidance.** Choose “Daftar”; enter a unique fictitious “Nama pengguna”, “Surel” and “Kata sandi”, and choose “Buat akun”. Then use “Masuk”. In “Profil tahun pajak”, enter a fictitious “Nama”, choose “Tidak punya NIB” for “Status NIB”, enter `47111` for “KBLI” and `Penjualan sembako` for “Uraian usaha”. Select “Tidak kawin” for “Status perpajakan pasangan” and “Ya” for “Wajib pajak baru tahun ini?”, then choose “Simpan”. Show the resulting “Panduan untuk profil Anda” panel and its citations. Use “Keluar”, then sign in to the seeded free account with “Surel atau nama pengguna” and “Kata sandi” for the next steps.
2. **Manual transaction and classification.** Choose “Catat transaksi”. Enter `2026-10-15` for “Tanggal transaksi”, `PT Sumber Makmur` for “Nama klien atau lawan transaksi”, `Penjualan sembako` for “Deskripsi layanan atau barang”, `1000000` for “Nominal bruto (omzet tercatat)”, and `INV-DEMO-MANUAL` as the invoice. Choose “Simpan dan klasifikasikan”. The detail shows the recorded **Rp 1.000.000**, “Kategori yang ditetapkan” as Usaha and the source of classification. This transaction belongs to the free account and does not change the paid-account figures below.
3. **Clarification.** Open “Daftar transaksi”, find invoice `INV-G-003`, and open it. The detail says “Belum terklasifikasi” and presents a question with four choices. Choose “Penjualan barang atau usaha” (matching the grocery store profile). “Pertanyaan sudah dijawab” appears with “Baris ini sekarang tercatat sebagai Usaha, dan sumbernya sudah berganti ke Klarifikasi.”, and “Kategori yang ditetapkan” updates to Usaha. If AI is unavailable, this rule-based question is the fallback; no live AI is needed to show it.
4. **Receipt OCR.** Sign out and sign in to the paid account. On “Catat transaksi pendapatan”, choose “Unggah bukti transaksi”, supply AI consent when prompted, and choose a fictitious JPEG or PNG receipt. This needs live keys, see #29. Review the extracted fields; saving confirms them and creates a transaction. For the exact dashboard figures below, cancel without saving. If OCR is unavailable, show “Unggah bukti transaksi” and explain that the same fields can be entered manually; do not imply that OCR ran.
5. **Dashboard thresholds.** Open the dashboard on the paid account without saving a new transaction. “Sisa omzet bebas pajak” is **Rp 0** of the **Rp 500.000.000** business band. The recorded usaha omzet is **Rp 3.925.000.000**. The “Omzet gabungan mendekati batas tahun berjalan” notice reports **Rp 3.932.500.000** combined against **Rp 4.800.000.000**. The seeded compute path reports total final tax of **Rp 17.125.000** across its monthly rows. In the monthly table, September tax is **Rp 17.000.000** and October tax is **Rp 125.000**, which was already withheld at source, so the amount still to pay (“Kurang bayar” / “sisa”) is **Rp 17.000.000**. These amounts come from the app's academic ruleset and seeded records, not live tax advice.
6. **Workpaper PDF.** On the paid dashboard choose “Unduh workpaper”. Open the PDF and show the ruleset, monthly lines and workpaper disclaimer. The PDF is a working paper, not a filed return.
7. **Assistant.** Open the assistant, enter `Kenapa transaksi saya masuk kategori pekerjaan bebas, bukan usaha?` in “Pertanyaan Anda” and choose “Kirim pertanyaan”. This needs live keys, see #29. If unavailable, show the assistant page and its “Asisten hanya membaca” explanation or the service-unavailable message; explain that it reads existing results rather than computing them.
8. **Subscription purchase.** Sign back in to the free account, open “Tingkat akun dan paket”, and choose “Buat tautan bayar”. This needs live keys, see #30. On current `main`, the Midtrans dashboard Finish URL must be configured manually to return to `/langganan/kembali`; that manual step is expected to go away once PR #55 merges. If Midtrans is unavailable, show the plan and the unavailable message; do not claim a payment succeeded. The seeded paid account can be used to show paid-only screens without a purchase.

The application is not an SPT, does not calculate Pasal 17, and is not DJP software.
